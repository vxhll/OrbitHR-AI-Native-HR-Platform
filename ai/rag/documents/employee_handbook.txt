ORBIT HR - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Employee Support

Employees can contact HR for questions concerning workplace policies, benefits, leave, attendance, and employee processes.

2. Workplace Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain professional and respectful behavior toward colleagues, managers, customers, and business partners.

3. Performance Reviews

Employees participate in periodic performance reviews.

Performance discussions may cover achievements, development areas, goals, responsibilities, and career growth.

4. Career Development

Employees are encouraged to discuss career development goals with their managers.

Development opportunities may include training, mentoring, expanded responsibilities, and internal opportunities.

5. Grievances

Employees may raise workplace concerns through their manager or the HR function.

Employees should provide sufficient information for HR to understand and review the concern.

6. Confidentiality

Employee information should be handled appropriately and accessed only by authorized individuals for legitimate business purposes.
