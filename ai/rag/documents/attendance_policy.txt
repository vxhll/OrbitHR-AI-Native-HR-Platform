ORBIT HR - ATTENDANCE POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Working Hours

Standard working hours are determined by the employee's assigned work schedule.

Employees are expected to be available during their scheduled working hours.

2. Attendance Recording

Employees must record attendance through the organization's approved attendance system.

Attendance records should accurately represent the employee's working activity.

3. Late Arrival

Employees who expect to arrive late should inform their manager as early as reasonably possible.

Repeated attendance issues may be reviewed by the employee's manager and HR.

4. Remote Work

Remote work is permitted only when supported by the organization's applicable work arrangement and manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their scheduled working hours.

5. Attendance Issues

Attendance concerns should first be discussed between the employee and their manager.

HR may become involved when attendance issues are repeated or require formal review.
