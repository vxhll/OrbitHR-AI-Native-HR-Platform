ORBIT HR - LEAVE POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Casual Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Casual leave may be used for personal work, short-term requirements, or unforeseen situations.

Employees should submit casual leave requests through the HR system whenever possible.

2. Sick Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible when taking sick leave.

Medical documentation may be requested for extended periods of absence.

3. Earned Leave

Employees are eligible for 18 days of earned leave per calendar year.

Earned leave should generally be requested in advance and is subject to manager approval.

4. Leave Approval

Leave requests are reviewed by the employee's reporting manager.

Approval depends on team requirements, planned workload, and applicable leave balances.

5. Unplanned Leave

In emergencies or unexpected situations, employees should notify their manager as soon as possible.

The employee should submit the corresponding leave request after returning to work if advance submission was not possible.
