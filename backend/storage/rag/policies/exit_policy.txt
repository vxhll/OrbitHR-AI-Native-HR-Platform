ORBIT HR - EXIT POLICY

Policy ID: HR-EXIT-001
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Policy Type: Employee Exit
Status: Active
Scope: Employees completing employment

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the general employee exit process, including resignation, notice period, approvals, handover, asset return, and exit documentation.

This is a synthetic policy created for the OrbitHR demonstration platform.

2. RESIGNATION

Employees who wish to resign should submit their resignation through the organization's designated HR process.

The resignation record should include the intended last working date where applicable.

3. NOTICE PERIOD

The applicable notice period depends on the employee's employment terms and organizational rules.

Employees should refer to their employment agreement or contact HR for the applicable notice period.

4. APPROVAL

A resignation is processed through the designated HR workflow.

The reporting manager and HR may be involved in reviewing the resignation and exit timeline.

5. HANDOVER

Employees are expected to complete an appropriate handover before their last working day.

The handover may include:

- Current responsibilities
- Projects
- Documentation
- Pending work
- Relevant contacts
- System information where authorized

6. COMPANY ASSETS

Employees must return applicable company assets before or during the exit process.

Examples include:

- Laptop
- Access cards
- Company equipment
- Other assigned property

7. ACCESS REVOCATION

Company system access may be reviewed and revoked as part of the employee exit process.

Access management should follow authorized security procedures.

8. EXIT INTERVIEW

HR may conduct an exit interview to understand employee experience, reasons for leaving, and opportunities for organizational improvement.

Exit feedback may be analyzed in aggregate.

9. FINAL SETTLEMENT

Final payroll or settlement processing is handled according to the organization's applicable procedures and employee records.

Employees should contact HR or payroll regarding settlement questions.

10. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How do I resign?

A: Submit your resignation through the designated HR process.

Q: What is my notice period?

A: The applicable notice period depends on your employment terms and organizational rules. Contact HR if clarification is required.

Q: Do I need to complete a handover?

A: Employees are expected to complete an appropriate handover before their last working day.

Q: What happens to company equipment?

A: Applicable company assets must be returned during the exit process.

Q: Can exit interviews be conducted?

A: Yes. HR may conduct an exit interview to understand employee experience and identify improvement opportunities.

11. POLICY CONTACT

Employees should contact HR for clarification regarding resignation, notice period, handover, or final settlement.
