ORBIT HR - WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Policy ID: HR-WFH-001
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Policy Type: Remote Work
Status: Active
Scope: Eligible full-time employees

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the requirements and approval process for working from home.

This is a synthetic policy created for the OrbitHR demonstration platform.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Work from home availability depends on the employee's role, department, operational requirements, and approved organizational arrangements.

Not every role is eligible for remote work.

3. APPROVAL

Employees must obtain approval through the organization's designated workflow before working from home when prior approval is required.

A work from home request is not automatically approved after submission.

4. REQUEST PROCESS

A request should normally include:

- Requested date
- Duration
- Reason where required
- Work location
- Any relevant operational information

The reporting manager reviews the request.

HR may review requests where required.

5. AVAILABILITY

Employees working from home must remain available during their approved working hours and maintain expected communication with their team.

6. WORK OUTPUT

Employees working from home are expected to meet the same role-related responsibilities and performance expectations applicable to their position.

7. SECURITY

Employees working remotely must protect company information and use approved systems, devices, and access methods.

Confidential company information should not be exposed to unauthorized individuals.

8. CANCELLATION

An approved work from home arrangement may be changed or cancelled when operational requirements require it.

Employees should inform their reporting manager of relevant changes.

9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Can every employee work from home?

A: No. Eligibility depends on the employee's role, department, operational requirements, and approved organizational arrangements.

Q: Does submitting a work from home request mean it is approved?

A: No. Approval is required where the policy requires prior approval.

Q: Who normally reviews a work from home request?

A: The reporting manager normally reviews the request. HR may review it when required.

Q: Are employees expected to remain available while working from home?

A: Yes. Employees are expected to remain available during approved working hours.

10. POLICY CONTACT

Employees should contact HR for clarification about work from home eligibility or approval requirements.
