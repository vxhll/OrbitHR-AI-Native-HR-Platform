ORBIT HR - PAYROLL POLICY

Policy ID: HR-PAY-001
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Policy Type: Payroll
Status: Active
Scope: All full-time employees

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines general payroll processing, salary information, deductions, attendance inputs, and payroll issue reporting.

This is a synthetic policy created for the OrbitHR demonstration platform.

2. PAYROLL CYCLE

Payroll is processed according to the organization's configured monthly payroll cycle.

Employees should ensure that required employee information is accurate and up to date.

3. SALARY

Employee salary information is maintained in the organization's HR system.

Salary information is confidential and should only be accessible to authorized employees and HR personnel.

4. ATTENDANCE INPUT

Attendance and approved leave information may be used as inputs to payroll processing.

Corrections to attendance or leave records should be completed through the designated HR process.

5. DEDUCTIONS

Applicable deductions may be reflected in payroll according to employee records and organizational requirements.

Employees should contact HR or the payroll team when clarification is required.

6. PAYROLL INFORMATION

Employees should keep relevant personal and banking information updated in the HR system.

Incorrect information may affect payroll processing.

7. PAYROLL ISSUE REPORTING

Employees who identify a potential payroll issue should raise the issue through the designated HR or payroll support process.

The issue may require verification of employee, attendance, leave, or payroll records.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees must not disclose another employee's confidential salary information.

Authorized HR and payroll personnel may access salary information for legitimate business purposes.

9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Is attendance used in payroll processing?

A: Attendance and approved leave information may be used as payroll inputs.

Q: Who can access salary information?

A: Salary information should only be accessible to authorized employees and HR personnel.

Q: What should I do if my salary information appears incorrect?

A: Raise the issue through the designated HR or payroll support process.

Q: Should my banking information be kept updated?

A: Yes. Employees should keep relevant banking information updated in the HR system.

10. POLICY CONTACT

Employees should contact HR or the payroll team for payroll-specific clarification.
