ORBIT HR - ATTENDANCE POLICY

Policy ID: HR-ATT-001
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Policy Type: Attendance
Status: Active
Scope: All full-time employees

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines attendance expectations, working hours, login requirements, late arrival, absence reporting, and attendance correction procedures.

This is a synthetic policy created for the OrbitHR demonstration platform.

2. STANDARD WORKING HOURS

Employees are expected to follow the working hours assigned to their department, role, location, and employment arrangement.

The standard working schedule is maintained in the organization's HR system.

3. ATTENDANCE RECORDING

Employees must record attendance using the organization's designated attendance or login system.

Attendance records may be used for workforce management, payroll processing, compliance, and reporting.

4. LATE ARRIVAL

Employees arriving after their scheduled start time should inform their reporting manager when required.

Repeated late arrivals may be reviewed by the reporting manager and HR.

5. ABSENCE

Employees who cannot attend work should inform their reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.

An absence without appropriate notification may be treated as an unauthorized absence subject to HR review.

6. ATTENDANCE CORRECTION

If an employee believes that an attendance record is incorrect, the employee should submit an attendance correction request through the designated HR system.

The request may require manager or HR approval.

7. REMOTE WORK ATTENDANCE

Employees working remotely must follow the attendance and login requirements applicable to their approved work arrangement.

8. UNAUTHORIZED ABSENCE

Unauthorized absence may be reviewed by HR and the reporting manager.

Any resulting action must follow applicable company procedures and human review.

9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How should I report an absence?

A: Inform your reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible and follow the designated HR process.

Q: What should I do if my attendance record is incorrect?

A: Submit an attendance correction request through the designated HR system.

Q: Can repeated late arrivals be reviewed?

A: Yes. Repeated late arrivals may be reviewed by the reporting manager and HR.

Q: Is attendance used for payroll processing?

A: Attendance records may be used as an input for payroll processing.

10. POLICY CONTACT

Employees should contact HR for clarification regarding attendance situations not covered by this policy.
