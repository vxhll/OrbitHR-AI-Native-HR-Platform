ORBIT HR - ONBOARDING POLICY

Policy ID: HR-ONB-001
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Policy Type: Employee Onboarding
Status: Active
Scope: Newly hired employees

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the onboarding process for newly hired employees, including required documentation, tasks, verification, and readiness.

This is a synthetic policy created for the OrbitHR demonstration platform.

2. ONBOARDING CASE

An onboarding case is created for a newly hired employee.

The onboarding case tracks tasks, required documents, progress, and readiness.

3. ADAPTIVE TASKS

Onboarding tasks may vary based on:

- Department
- Job role
- Job level
- Employee responsibilities

Common HR tasks may be combined with role-specific tasks.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

The standard onboarding process may require:

- Identity Proof
- Address Proof
- Bank Account Details
- Tax Declaration

Additional documentation may be requested when required.

5. DOCUMENT SUBMISSION

Employees must submit required documents through the designated HR system.

Uploaded documents are stored according to the organization's security and access controls.

6. DOCUMENT VERIFICATION

HR or an authorized reviewer verifies submitted documents.

A document may be:

- Pending
- Submitted
- Verified
- Rejected

Rejected documents may require re-submission.

7. ONBOARDING TASKS

Employees must complete the tasks assigned to their onboarding case.

Tasks may include:

- Completing the employee profile
- Acknowledging the employee handbook
- Security awareness training
- Meeting HR
- Role-specific setup
- Manager orientation

8. ONBOARDING READINESS

An employee is onboarding-ready when all required onboarding tasks are completed and all required documents are verified.

The HR system calculates onboarding completion and readiness.

9. HUMAN REVIEW

Sensitive onboarding decisions requiring HR judgment must remain subject to human review.

The system should not automatically make employment decisions without appropriate authorization.

10. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What documents are normally required?

A: Identity Proof, Address Proof, Bank Account Details, and Tax Declaration are standard onboarding documents.

Q: Can onboarding tasks differ between employees?

A: Yes. Tasks may vary based on department, job role, and job level.

Q: What happens if a document is rejected?

A: The employee may need to re-submit the required document.

Q: When is an employee onboarding-ready?

A: When all required tasks are completed and all required documents are verified.

11. POLICY CONTACT

Employees should contact HR for onboarding questions or clarification.
