ORBIT HR — LEAVE POLICY

Policy ID: HR-LEAVE-001
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Policy Type: Employee Leave
Status: Active
Scope: All full-time employees

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the types of leave available to employees, eligibility requirements, approval rules, and general procedures for requesting leave.

This is a synthetic policy created for the OrbitHR demonstration platform.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid leave per calendar year.

The annual leave entitlement is allocated as follows:

- Casual Leave: 6 days
- Sick Leave: 6 days
- Earned Leave: 12 days

Unused Casual Leave and Sick Leave do not automatically carry forward to the next calendar year.

Earned Leave may be carried forward subject to the organization's applicable leave rules.

3. CASUAL LEAVE

Casual Leave is intended for short-duration personal requirements.

Employees may request Casual Leave for:

- Personal appointments
- Family requirements
- Urgent personal matters
- Short-term personal commitments

Casual Leave should normally be requested at least one working day in advance.

For unexpected situations, the employee should inform the reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.

4. SICK LEAVE

Sick Leave is intended for illness, medical recovery, or health-related situations.

Employees should notify their reporting manager when they are unable to attend work due to illness.

A medical certificate may be requested for extended periods of sick leave or where required by HR.

5. EARNED LEAVE

Earned Leave is intended for planned longer-duration absence.

Employees should normally submit an Earned Leave request at least seven calendar days before the planned leave start date.

Earned Leave requests are subject to manager approval and operational requirements.

6. LEAVE REQUEST PROCESS

Employees must submit leave requests through the organization's designated HR or leave management system.

A leave request should contain:

- Leave type
- Start date
- End date
- Number of leave days
- Reason, where required

The reporting manager is responsible for reviewing the request.

HR may review leave requests when required by policy or compliance procedures.

7. LEAVE APPROVAL

Submitting a leave request does not automatically mean that the leave has been approved.

Leave becomes approved only after the designated approver accepts the request.

Employees should not assume that a pending leave request is approved.

8. EXTENDED LEAVE

Leave extending beyond normal entitlement requires additional HR review.

Extended leave may require supporting documentation and approval from both the reporting manager and HR.

9. HOLIDAYS AND WEEKENDS

Organization holidays and weekly non-working days are handled according to the applicable organizational calendar and leave configuration.

The HR system determines the applicable leave-day calculation.

10. LEAVE CANCELLATION

Employees may request cancellation of an approved future leave period.

Cancellation is subject to manager approval and operational requirements.

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How many paid leave days are available per year?

A: Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid leave per calendar year.

Q: How many Casual Leave days are available?

A: Employees receive 6 Casual Leave days per calendar year.

Q: How many Sick Leave days are available?

A: Employees receive 6 Sick Leave days per calendar year.

Q: How many Earned Leave days are available?

A: Employees receive 12 Earned Leave days per calendar year.

Q: Does submitting a leave request mean it is approved?

A: No. A leave request must be approved by the designated approver.

Q: How early should Earned Leave normally be requested?

A: Earned Leave should normally be requested at least seven calendar days before the planned start date.

12. POLICY CONTACT

Employees should contact HR for clarification when a situation is not covered by this policy.
